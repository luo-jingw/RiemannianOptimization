## Purpose

What this project is for and who it serves.

A narrated explainer video that rebuilds the EECE7223 Riemannian Optimization course material from the foundations, for a student catching up on the course. Course sources live in `notes/`; the knowledge structure is in `docs/knowledge-map.md`.

## Environment

- Platform / hardware: WSL2 Ubuntu 22.04 on Windows; 20 CPU threads; NVIDIA RTX 4060 Laptop 8 GB (CUDA, used by Kokoro and faster-whisper); headless Chrome renders WebGL through ANGLE/D3D12 on the Intel iGPU.
- Shared with (other users or projects on the same account or machine): the GPU also hosts other workloads (~3 GB in use at setup).
- Constraints imposed by the environment: no system espeak-ng, poppler or Chinese fonts beyond Droid Sans Fallback — fonts come from npm (@fontsource), PDFs are read with PyMuPDF; Python 3.11 venv at `.venv` (uv) holds torch 2.6 cu124, kokoro 0.9.4, misaki 0.9.4, spaCy en_core_web_sm 3.8.0, faster-whisper; Node 24 + npm packages under `player/node_modules`; ffmpeg 4.4 (`loudnorm` needs `aformat=channel_layouts=stereo` at the end of the chain for 24-bit WAV input).

## Project-Specific Constraints

Rules that apply only to this project, in addition to `AGENTS.md`.

- `notes/` is read-only source material.
- Narration: English, Kokoro TTS, female voice.
- Subtitles: bilingual, English line above Chinese line.
- Background music: original procedural score (`pipeline/rvideo/music/`); each episode's key follows the circle of fifths by episode order.
- Every theorem is presented with motivation, complete proof, and an animated counterexample for each hypothesis.
- At most 3 episode builds run concurrently, each with one heavy process (TTS, transcription or headless Chrome) at a time: six concurrent builds froze the WSL VM (23 GB RAM).
- Delivered versions under `output/<eid>/v<N>/` are never overwritten; a change produces a new version.
