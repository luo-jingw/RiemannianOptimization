# Trailer asset sources

Every file under `player/public/trailer/` is listed here with its origin, licence and checksum. Derived files
are regenerated by the scripts in `pipeline/rvideo/assets/` (run as `.venv/bin/python -I -m rvideo.assets.<name>`)
from raw downloads kept in `build/trailer-assets/raw/<name>/`. All scripts are deterministic: a re-run reproduces
the SHA-256 values below.

## File formats

### `dti-slice.json` (script `dti_slice.py`)

Axial slice 37 of the 81×106×76 (2 mm isotropic) Stanford HARDI volume, b = 2000 s/mm², 150 directions.
Brain mask: `median_otsu(vol_idx=10..49, median_radius=3, numpass=1)`. Tensors: `dipy.reconst.dti.TensorModel`
(weighted least squares). Downsampling: Log-Euclidean mean of 2×2 blocks of tensors, giving a 40×53 grid at
4 mm spacing; a block is kept when the Gaussian-smoothed (σ = 1 voxel) mask averages at least 0.5 over it.

- `frame`: RAS; x = subject right (+i), y = anterior (+j), z = superior, out of the slice; right-handed.
- `grid` = [40, 53]; `voxel_spacing_mm` = [4, 4].
- `voxels[]` (1017 entries): `i`, `j`, `fa`, `evals` (descending, divided by `eigenvalue_scale_mm2_per_s`
  = 2.377e-3 mm²/s, the largest eigenvalue over the slice), `evecs` (three unit 3-vectors in the frame above;
  `evecs[n]` belongs to `evals[n]`; `evecs[2] = evecs[0] × evecs[1]`).
- `outline.outer`: brain silhouette, closed polygon (counter-clockwise, last point not repeated) from marching
  squares at level 0.5 of the smoothed mask, in grid-index units (voxel (i, j) is centred at (i, j)).
- `outline.holes`: interior loops (clockwise); here one loop around the lateral ventricles, which the mask excludes.

The largest eigenvalues come from CSF-adjacent voxels: normalized λ₁ has median 0.37 and 95th percentile 0.66.

### `pca-cloud.json` (script `pca_cloud.py`)

All 13 611 beans, 16 shape features z-scored; projected onto the top three principal components
(16-D explained variance ratios 0.555, 0.264, 0.080). Within each variety, points whose Mahalanobis distance
in the 3-D score space exceeds the variety's 98th percentile are dropped; then 340 beans per variety
(7 varieties, 2380 points) are drawn with seed 7223. The points are centred, scaled so the 99th-percentile
radius is 1, and rotated by Euler XYZ (−62°, 18°, 25°) so the dominant plane is oblique to the axes.

- `points[]`: [x, y, z]; `labels[]`: index into `class_names`.
- `principal_directions`: the top two right singular vectors of the exported, centred points;
  `singular_values`: their singular values (17.77, 10.66); `singular_values_all3` adds the third (4.84).

### `models/ur5e.glb` + `models/ur5e-rig.json` (script `ur5e_model.py`)

Metres, y-up. The root node `ur5e` rotates MuJoCo's z-up frame to y-up. Each MJCF body becomes a node with its
fixed pose; each joint becomes a child node with an identity rest transform, named after the joint
(`shoulder_pan_joint`, `shoulder_lift_joint`, `elbow_joint`, `wrist_1_joint`, `wrist_2_joint`, `wrist_3_joint`),
and the link meshes hang below it. A joint angle θ is applied by setting that joint node's quaternion to a rotation
of θ about the joint's `axis` from `ur5e-rig.json` (local frame). The rig file also gives the joint ranges and the
home pose (−π/2, −π/2, π/2, −π/2, −π/2, 0). Meshes: welded, decimated to 60 % of their faces
(`fast_simplification`), with normals split at creases sharper than 35°; 75 300 triangles in total. Four PBR
materials keep the MJCF colours (black, jointgray, linkgray, urblue).

### `models/skydio-x2.glb` (script `x2_model.py`)

Metres, y-up. The root node `skydio_x2` rotates z-up to y-up. The OBJ is placed as in `x2.xml` (scale 0.01,
geom quaternion 0 0 1 1); the original normals and UVs are kept, along with one embedded 1024² base-colour texture.
Node `body` holds 2234 triangles. Nodes `rotor1`–`rotor4` (numbered as in `x2.xml`) each hold one flat
80-triangle disc, which the texture paints as a motion-blurred spinning propeller. Each disc node is translated to
its disc centre and spins about its local +z.
